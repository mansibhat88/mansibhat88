# Hi, I'm Mansi 👋

I help teams decide what to automate, where AI is worth testing, and how to tell whether it's working.

I'm at my best when the business question is still messy. I work with leaders to clarify the decision, with technical teams to figure out what's feasible, and with the people doing the work to understand what would make their day easier.

### How I approach an AI pilot

Say a team wants to automate a weekly report. I'd ask, in this order:

1. What can rules handle?
2. What might AI help draft?
3. What does someone still need to verify?
4. What would a pilot need to show before anyone relies on it?
